Before you play

An unofficial fan project · version 1.0

  • Not official. This is a hobby project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Activision or Infocom. Zork is a trademark of its owner.
  • Bring your own disk. Z3 Multiboot is a player. Drop a version 3 story file onto the page, such as Zork I, II or III, which Microsoft released under the MIT licence in 2025, or most other Infocom games up to 1985.
  • The computers are impressions. The Commodore 64, Macintosh, Apple II, IBM PC, Amiga and NeXTSTEP looks are AI-made likenesses built for fun. They are not the original systems or software, and the makers of those machines have nothing to do with this.
  • New here? Boot the Welcome Disk from the empty-drive screen: a short adventure that shows you around.
  • Need a disk? Microsoft released Zork I, II and III on GitHub at historicalsource. Look for the .z3 file in each game's COMPILED folder. The file stays in your browser.
  • Saves stay in this browser. Use Export in Saves to keep a copy, and Save transcript to keep a record of your game.
  • Changed your mind? Type UNDO to take back your last turn, up to 20 turns in a row.

Have fun, and keep an eye out for the easter eggs, both the obvious ones and the not so obvious.
